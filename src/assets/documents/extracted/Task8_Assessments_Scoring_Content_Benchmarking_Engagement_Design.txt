RETIREMENTTRACK
Task 8 – Assessments, Scoring, Content, Benchmarking & Engagement Design
Discovery deliverable defining the functional and technical approach for client assessments, scoring/results, Planning Updates, educational-content personalization, tagging, benchmarking, reminder rules, advisor-controlled communications, and the recurring engagement model.
# 1. Purpose & Design Principles
Task 8 converts the product direction established in Discovery into a defined engagement design that can be implemented consistently across the client experience, advisor experience, domain model, and technical architecture.
Principle
Recommended Direction
Self-directed client experience
RetirementTrack should surface appropriate activities, reminders, milestones, assessments, Planning Updates and education without requiring routine advisor assignment.
High advisor visibility, low routine workload
Advisor dashboards must separate Engagement/Accomplishments, Outstanding Client Items and Advisor Attention/Exceptions.
Explainable personalization
MVP recommendations and priorities should be deterministic, rules-based and traceable. No AI recommendation engine for MVP.
Versioned business logic
Assessments, scoring, Planning Updates, benchmark data/methodology, templates and important engagement rules must retain version history.
Data minimization
Collect only the client/household information necessary for approved workflows, calculations, personalization and benchmarking.
Professional judgment boundary
RetirementTrack identifies topics, changes and conditions but does not provide individualized financial or investment recommendations.
MVP discipline
Build a focused, credible engagement loop now while preserving clean extension points for future analytics, proprietary benchmarks, integrations and richer content management.

# 3. Assessment Framework
Recommendation: use a small, fixed and versioned assessment inventory for the MVP. Current working examples are a fuller client questionnaire and a condensed Investment & Risk Assessment. Do not build a generalized form/assessment builder.
Area
Recommended MVP Design
Definition
AssessmentDefinition identifies the stable assessment type; AssessmentVersion contains the exact questions, scoring and outputs for a release.
Question types
Single choice, multiple choice, yes/no, numeric/range, date, and limited free text where business value is clear.
Conditional logic
Support simple predefined branching; avoid arbitrary scripts or visual rule builders.
Availability
Firm may enable approved assessment types. RetirementTrack may automatically surface selected assessments based on defined rules; advisors may also recommend/assign supported assessments.
Lifecycle
Available → Assigned/Started → In Progress → Submitted → Scored → Completed; optional Reviewed state for advisor workflow.
Save/resume
Support save and resume for longer assessments.
Repeatability
Allow repeat assessments where the assessment definition permits it. Each completion remains tied to its original version.
History
Never silently reinterpret a historical result using a newer assessment version.

# 19. Retirement Journey & Visible Progress
RetirementTrack should present client progress as a coherent retirement journey rather than only as a list of completed and outstanding activities. The experience should help the client understand what has been addressed, what remains, which milestones are approaching, and where additional attention may be useful.
Journey View
Recommended Design
MVP Boundary
Addressed
Summarize meaningful accomplishments such as completed assessments, Planning Updates, checklists, reviewed milestones and other relevant engagement.

Remaining
Show important outstanding Planning Updates, assessments, milestones or planning areas without turning every item into advisor work.
Prioritized, bounded presentation rather than a comprehensive task inventory.
Approaching

Rules-based and based on validated milestone definitions.
Attention useful

Preserve an extension point for richer progress/readiness presentation later without introducing a new generalized scoring engine in the MVP.
# 20. Pre-Retirement & Post-Retirement Engagement
The recurring engagement model must remain useful on both sides of retirement. Before retirement the client question is “Am I prepared?” During retirement it becomes “Am I staying on track?” RetirementTrack should support both through the same bounded, rules-based engagement architecture.
Stage / Trigger
Illustrative Engagement
Recommended Handling
Before retirement
Preparedness activities, retirement timing, Medicare/Social Security preparation, assessments, Planning Updates and approaching milestones.
Surface priorities, education and progress; create advisor attention only for approved exceptions or client requests.
During retirement - withdrawals
Periodic withdrawal/draw-rate evaluation prompts and coordination of RMD distributions with annual portfolio cash-flow needs.
Identify a review consideration; surface education or encourage advisor discussion rather than calculate an individualized recommendation.
During retirement - RMD
RMD preparation, reminders, completion and related annual planning prompts.
Use validated age/calendar rules, bounded reminders and completion tracking.
During retirement - tax / Medicare
Year-end consideration of tax-loss opportunities in non-qualified brokerage accounts; Medicare and IRMAA-related planning considerations.
Present timely planning observations/education; professional review when an approved rule warrants it.
During retirement - giving / protection
QCD or charitable-giving opportunities where applicable; beneficiary, estate and insurance reviews.
Surface relevant education/checklists and optional advisor contact or approved exception.
During retirement - circumstances
Changes in income or spending needs and other age-, calendar-, market-, tax- or circumstance-driven retirement considerations.
Update priorities/content/reminders; create AdvisorException only when an explicit approved rule is met.
These interactions identify issues, considerations or planning opportunities. They may direct the client to educational material, prompt a review, encourage advisor contact, or create an AdvisorException under an approved rule; they do not provide individualized financial, investment, tax or legal recommendations.
## Freshness / Discovery Engagement
RetirementTrack should continue to provide discovery value even when the client has no required task or immediate milestone. Curated educational resources, timely planning observations, factoids and similar content may be surfaced using controlled tags and deterministic rules based on appropriate client context and prior activity. This remains bounded engagement logic - not a broad marketing-automation platform or AI recommendation engine.

# 27. Confirmed Business Rules
Grant confirmed the following business/product rules for the MVP. These decisions close the six open confirmation items while preserving versioned, rules-based implementation.
#
Decision
Recommended Default for MVP
1
Assessment retake / repeat policy
Allow repeat assessments according to the defined cadence for that assessment, by advisor request, or following an appropriate material client change. Avoid unrestricted retakes.
2
Historical assessment comparison
Advisor sees meaningful prior/current comparison; client receives a simpler change indication where useful.
3
Assessment exception rules
Only explicitly configured material results or threshold changes create an Advisor Attention/Exception. Thresholds are defined appropriately for each assessment.
4
Content completion semantics
Use content-specific completion: video = watched threshold; checklist = explicit completion; article/guide = viewed/opened unless richer tracking is later justified.
5
Benchmark recalculation
Preserve each historical result using the dataset and methodology applicable at that time; calculate a new current comparison against a newly validated benchmark dataset.
6
Dismiss / snooze behavior
Informational and educational items may generally be snoozed or dismissed. Core required Planning Updates and assessments remain outstanding until completed or administratively removed.
# 28. Final Recommended Engagement Boundary
