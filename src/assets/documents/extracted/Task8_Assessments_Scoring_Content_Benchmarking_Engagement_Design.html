RETIREMENTTRACK

Task 8 – Assessments, Scoring, Content, Benchmarking & Engagement Design

Discovery deliverable defining the functional and technical approach for client assessments, scoring/results, Planning Updates, educational-content personalization, tagging, benchmarking, reminder rules, advisor-controlled communications, and the recurring engagement model.

1. Purpose & Design Principles

Task 8 converts the product direction established in Discovery into a defined engagement design that can be implemented consistently across the client experience, advisor experience, domain model, and technical architecture.

PrincipleRecommended Direction
Self-directed client experienceRetirementTrack should surface appropriate activities, reminders, milestones, assessments, Planning Updates and education without requiring routine advisor assignment.
High advisor visibility, low routine workloadAdvisor dashboards must separate Engagement/Accomplishments, Outstanding Client Items and Advisor Attention/Exceptions.
Explainable personalizationMVP recommendations and priorities should be deterministic, rules-based and traceable. No AI recommendation engine for MVP.
Versioned business logicAssessments, scoring, Planning Updates, benchmark data/methodology, templates and important engagement rules must retain version history.
Data minimizationCollect only the client/household information necessary for approved workflows, calculations, personalization and benchmarking.
Professional judgment boundaryRetirementTrack identifies topics, changes and conditions but does not provide individualized financial or investment recommendations.
MVP disciplineBuild a focused, credible engagement loop now while preserving clean extension points for future analytics, proprietary benchmarks, integrations and richer content management.

3. Assessment Framework

Recommendation: use a small, fixed and versioned assessment inventory for the MVP. Current working examples are a fuller client questionnaire and a condensed Investment & Risk Assessment. Do not build a generalized form/assessment builder.

AreaRecommended MVP Design
DefinitionAssessmentDefinition identifies the stable assessment type; AssessmentVersion contains the exact questions, scoring and outputs for a release.
Question typesSingle choice, multiple choice, yes/no, numeric/range, date, and limited free text where business value is clear.
Conditional logicSupport simple predefined branching; avoid arbitrary scripts or visual rule builders.
AvailabilityFirm may enable approved assessment types. RetirementTrack may automatically surface selected assessments based on defined rules; advisors may also recommend/assign supported assessments.
LifecycleAvailable → Assigned/Started → In Progress → Submitted → Scored → Completed; optional Reviewed state for advisor workflow.
Save/resumeSupport save and resume for longer assessments.
RepeatabilityAllow repeat assessments where the assessment definition permits it. Each completion remains tied to its original version.
HistoryNever silently reinterpret a historical result using a newer assessment version.

5. Scoring & Results

Do not force all assessments into one scoring shape. Each assessment may produce a numeric score, category/band, flags, educational result, or a combination of these.

OutputClient ExperienceAdvisor Experience
Numeric/category resultSimple, understandable explanation with caveats and next relevant action/content.Score/category plus important context and comparison to prior result where meaningful.
Flags / areas of attentionExplain topic without diagnosing or recommending individualized financial action.Show defined review reason if threshold is met.
No-score assessmentCompletion/accomplishment and relevant learning can still be shown.Visibility without forced advisor task.

6. Advisor Review / Exception Rules

Structural distinctionAssessment completion is an Engagement/Accomplishment. Incomplete or overdue assessment is an Outstanding Client Item. Only a defined review condition becomes an Advisor Attention/Exception.
ConditionRecommended Outcome
Normal completionRecord accomplishment; update client state; surface relevant education if appropriate.
Overdue/incompleteKeep as outstanding client item; RetirementTrack manages routine reminders.
Defined score/result thresholdCreate AdvisorException with reason, severity/priority and source assessment.
Client requests helpCreate client-initiated AdvisorException/FollowUp.
Advisor explicitly assigns follow-upCreate advisor-owned task with clear reason and due state.

7. Planning Update Design

Planning Update is a core recurring workflow, not a generic form. It should minimize re-entry by showing prior known information and asking the client to confirm or update it.

StepRecommended Behavior
Due generationTemporal workflow creates/activates the due instance according to cadence.
InvitationClient receives platform-led notification with secure link back to RetirementTrack.
Prior statePreviously provided values are shown where appropriate; client confirms or changes them.
Save/resumeIn-progress state persists safely.
SubmissionCurrent responses are committed as a versioned PlanningUpdateInstance.
Reminder stopSubmission/completion immediately ends remaining routine reminder sequence.
ComparisonDefined fields are compared to the prior completed state.
Change evaluationPlanningUpdateDetectedChange records relevant differences and whether advisor attention is warranted.
Dashboard refreshOutstanding item closes; progress, priorities and relevant content can change.
Advisor visibilityAdvisor sees completion and meaningful changes; advisor work is created only for true exceptions.

8. Meaningful Change Detection

Change ClassExamplesRecommended Handling
InformationalMinor update with no professional-review significance.History only; no advisor task.
RelevantLife-stage/context change that should alter dashboard/content/reminders.Update personalization and client state.
MeaningfulRetirement timing/status change, household change, material income/balance movement, estate/beneficiary/insurance signal, or other defined review condition.Create AdvisorException only if approved rule threshold is met.
Private individual changeInformation explicitly classified Individual Private.Respect household visibility boundary; expose only to authorized user/advisor according to policy.

Recommended implementation: rules are explicit and versioned. Store source field/reference, prior/current value or references where needed, rule/version, reason, severity, and AdvisorAttentionRequired flag. Avoid exposing detailed sensitive values on broad advisor dashboards; show concise status/reason and require authorized drill-down.

19. Retirement Journey & Visible Progress

RetirementTrack should present client progress as a coherent retirement journey rather than only as a list of completed and outstanding activities. The experience should help the client understand what has been addressed, what remains, which milestones are approaching, and where additional attention may be useful.

Journey ViewRecommended DesignMVP Boundary
AddressedSummarize meaningful accomplishments such as completed assessments, Planning Updates, checklists, reviewed milestones and other relevant engagement.Use existing engagement/accomplishment state; no separate scoring engine required.
RemainingShow important outstanding Planning Updates, assessments, milestones or planning areas without turning every item into advisor work.Prioritized, bounded presentation rather than a comprehensive task inventory.
ApproachingSurface upcoming age/date, retirement-stage and recurring planning milestones with an explanation of why they matter.Rules-based and based on validated milestone definitions.
Attention usefulIdentify areas where client review, education, advisor contact or an approved AdvisorException may be appropriate.Professional judgment remains with the advisor; no individualized recommendation.

Implementation direction: derive the journey/progress presentation from existing versioned engagement, assessment, Planning Update, milestone and outstanding-item state. Preserve an extension point for richer progress/readiness presentation later without introducing a new generalized scoring engine in the MVP.

20. Pre-Retirement & Post-Retirement Engagement

The recurring engagement model must remain useful on both sides of retirement. Before retirement the client question is “Am I prepared?” During retirement it becomes “Am I staying on track?” RetirementTrack should support both through the same bounded, rules-based engagement architecture.

Stage / TriggerIllustrative EngagementRecommended Handling
Before retirementPreparedness activities, retirement timing, Medicare/Social Security preparation, assessments, Planning Updates and approaching milestones.Surface priorities, education and progress; create advisor attention only for approved exceptions or client requests.
During retirement - withdrawalsPeriodic withdrawal/draw-rate evaluation prompts and coordination of RMD distributions with annual portfolio cash-flow needs.Identify a review consideration; surface education or encourage advisor discussion rather than calculate an individualized recommendation.
During retirement - RMDRMD preparation, reminders, completion and related annual planning prompts.Use validated age/calendar rules, bounded reminders and completion tracking.
During retirement - tax / MedicareYear-end consideration of tax-loss opportunities in non-qualified brokerage accounts; Medicare and IRMAA-related planning considerations.Present timely planning observations/education; professional review when an approved rule warrants it.
During retirement - giving / protectionQCD or charitable-giving opportunities where applicable; beneficiary, estate and insurance reviews.Surface relevant education/checklists and optional advisor contact or approved exception.
During retirement - circumstancesChanges in income or spending needs and other age-, calendar-, market-, tax- or circumstance-driven retirement considerations.Update priorities/content/reminders; create AdvisorException only when an explicit approved rule is met.

These interactions identify issues, considerations or planning opportunities. They may direct the client to educational material, prompt a review, encourage advisor contact, or create an AdvisorException under an approved rule; they do not provide individualized financial, investment, tax or legal recommendations.

Freshness / Discovery Engagement

RetirementTrack should continue to provide discovery value even when the client has no required task or immediate milestone. Curated educational resources, timely planning observations, factoids and similar content may be surfaced using controlled tags and deterministic rules based on appropriate client context and prior activity. This remains bounded engagement logic - not a broad marketing-automation platform or AI recommendation engine.

27. Confirmed Business Rules

Grant confirmed the following business/product rules for the MVP. These decisions close the six open confirmation items while preserving versioned, rules-based implementation.

#DecisionRecommended Default for MVP
1Assessment retake / repeat policyAllow repeat assessments according to the defined cadence for that assessment, by advisor request, or following an appropriate material client change. Avoid unrestricted retakes.
2Historical assessment comparisonAdvisor sees meaningful prior/current comparison; client receives a simpler change indication where useful.
3Assessment exception rulesOnly explicitly configured material results or threshold changes create an Advisor Attention/Exception. Thresholds are defined appropriately for each assessment.
4Content completion semanticsUse content-specific completion: video = watched threshold; checklist = explicit completion; article/guide = viewed/opened unless richer tracking is later justified.
5Benchmark recalculationPreserve each historical result using the dataset and methodology applicable at that time; calculate a new current comparison against a newly validated benchmark dataset.
6Dismiss / snooze behaviorInformational and educational items may generally be snoozed or dismissed. Core required Planning Updates and assessments remain outstanding until completed or administratively removed.

28. Final Recommended Engagement Boundary

Recommended first-build boundaryBuild a versioned, deterministic engagement system that supports an ongoing retirement relationship before and during retirement: surface a small number of relevant client priorities; run Planning Updates and fixed assessments; record accomplishments and outstanding items; present visible retirement-journey progress; surface contextual education and freshness/discovery content from a growing library; evaluate age/date and retirement-stage milestones; deliver bounded reminders and communications through Temporal Cloud; calculate a small validated set of external benchmarks; and create advisor exceptions only when professional attention is truly warranted. Post-retirement examples define supported extension patterns and do not make every illustrative workflow mandatory for the MVP.
