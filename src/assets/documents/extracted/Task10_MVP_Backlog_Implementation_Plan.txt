RETIREMENTTRACK
Task 10 – MVP Backlog, Implementation Plan & Estimate
# 1. Purpose & Executive Summary
This document converts the Discovery decisions into a practical implementation plan for the first production-ready RetirementTrack release. The objective is to preserve the product’s distinctive recurring-engagement model while keeping the first build focused, secure, supportable and commercially testable.
# 2. What the First Build Must Prove
MVP outcome
What success looks like
Firm-ready SaaS foundation
A firm can be configured, branded and securely isolated from every other firm.
Simple client onboarding
A client/household can be invited, activate access with MFA, complete minimum profile information and understand household sharing.
Personalized retirement journey
The client sees a small number of relevant priorities, visible progress, milestones and educational items rather than a generic task list.
Recurring engagement
Planning Updates, selected assessments, reminders, milestones and educational content create reasons to return before and during retirement.
Low routine advisor workload
Client accomplishments, outstanding items and true advisor exceptions are separate; routine engagement does not automatically create advisor work.
Professional-judgment boundary
The system identifies issues, considerations and opportunities but does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax or legal recommendations.
Pilot-operable platform
RetirementTrack owns production accounts; deployment, monitoring, backups, recovery, access and support procedures are ready for pilot use.
# 3. Prioritization Model
Priority
Meaning

Features that demonstrate RetirementTrack’s differentiated client/advisor engagement model.
Must be in the first usable MVP.
P2 – Pilot Important / Simplified
Needed for credible pilot operation but intentionally narrow/configured.
Build a simple version; avoid generalized engines.
P3 – Post-MVP / Future
Valuable capabilities that are not necessary to validate the first build.
Preserve extension points; do not build now.
