RETIREMENTTRACK

MVP Boundary & Prioritization

1. Prioritization Principles

Protect the core value proposition: A capability may be simplified, but the MVP should not remove personalization, recurring engagement, advisor exception management or the branded firm/client experience.

Relevant before comprehensive: Prefer a small number of useful priorities, content items and follow-up conditions over broad feature inventories or generic dashboards.

Rules before sophistication: Use explicit, understandable business rules for MVP personalization, relevance and follow-up rather than AI/ML, generalized rule builders or complex configurable engines.

Exceptions before monitoring: The advisor experience should surface clients who require attention and why, rather than require advisors to inspect every client or activity.

Professional judgment stays with the advisor: RetirementTrack can identify conditions, changes, planning topics and educational opportunities, but should not make individualized professional recommendations.

Credibility before differentiation claims: Benchmarking should be included only where the measure, source, cohort method, privacy treatment and explanatory caveats can be supported credibly.

Fixed first-build workflows before platforms: Use a narrow Planning Update, assessment and follow-up model before considering generalized form builders, workflow builders or campaign engines.

Architect for growth without building future scope: The data model, tenancy, permissions and integration seams should support future expansion, while implementation remains constrained to first-build needs.

2. Core MVP Value Proposition

Core proposition: RetirementTrack gives professional firms a branded, scalable way to keep retirement clients meaningfully engaged over time, while helping advisors quickly understand which clients need attention and why.

• For clients: a personalized experience that continually answers: Where am I? What deserves my attention? What should I do next?

• For advisors: an exception-driven view of meaningful changes, review needs, overdue items and defined follow-up conditions.

• For firms: a controlled, branded client experience with firm-level feature, communication, content and advisor/client governance.

• For the product: assessments, Planning Updates, reminders, relevant education, personalization and advisor follow-up work together as one recurring-engagement system rather than disconnected features.

3. MVP Engagement Loop

The first build should prove one coherent loop from client context to relevant action and back to updated client state.

First-build engagement loop: Firm / Advisor Setup → Client Invitation & Minimum Context → Personalized Dashboard → Relevant Activity or Trigger → Client Action → Advisor Attention Only When Needed → Updated Client State → Next Relevant Reason to Return

• Relevant activity or trigger may include a Planning Update, assessment, age/life-stage milestone, advisor-assigned item, reminder, newly relevant education or defined change/opportunity.

• Routine completion should update history and continuity without automatically creating advisor work.

• Meaningful changes or defined review conditions should create an advisor exception/follow-up item with a clear reason.

• The returning-client experience should change as state, completed work, assessments, milestones or advisor inputs change.

10. Benchmarking Decision Framework

Benchmarking is prioritized as a targeted MVP capability. The MVP should aim to include at least one simple, useful benchmark where Discovery validates a credible measure, data source, cohort method, privacy treatment and explanatory caveats. If those conditions cannot be met reliably, the architecture should preserve benchmarking readiness while implementation is deferred.

MVP rule: include a benchmark only when the measure, source, cohort logic, calculation, privacy treatment and client-facing caveat can all be validated during Discovery.
Decision TestMVP Inclusion Standard
Client valueThe comparison helps a client understand context or relative position and is understandable without specialist interpretation.
MeasureUse one or a small number of clearly defined measures; avoid a composite retirement-readiness score unless methodology is defensible.
Data sourceUse a credible, maintainable source such as curated public/reference data or another validated source. Do not depend on an unproven internal cohort.
Cohort definitionCohorts should be simple and explainable, for example age band or retirement stage, with enough data to avoid misleading precision.
PrivacyNo benchmark should expose another client or permit inference about small groups. Minimum cohort/privacy thresholds must be defined if platform data is ever used.
CalculationCalculation and refresh logic must be deterministic, testable and documented.
Caveat / interpretationPresent comparison as educational context, not a professional recommendation or prediction.
Firm controlFirm can enable/disable the benchmark; advisor/client visibility should follow agreed permissions.
FallbackIf required inputs or a validated benchmark are unavailable, show an unavailable/educational state rather than fabricate a comparison.

12. Advisor Automation vs Professional Judgment Boundary

RetirementTrack should automate detection, status, reminders and workflow cues while leaving individualized professional judgment with the advisor. The product should help the advisor know where attention is needed, not act as the advisor.

RetirementTrack may automateKeep advisor-controlled
Due dates, status transitions and completion tracking.Individualized financial, tax, investment, retirement-income or planning recommendations.
Reminder scheduling and approved communication modes.Interpretation of complex client circumstances where professional judgment is required.

Assessment scoring/category output where methodology is defined.Professional interpretation beyond the approved assessment result/context.

Exception queues that explain why attention is required.Automatic execution of professional decisions on behalf of the advisor.
